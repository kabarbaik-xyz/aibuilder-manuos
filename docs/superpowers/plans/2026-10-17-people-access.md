# People & Access implementation

Approved scope: connect employee onboarding, department, login role, technician qualifications, availability and assignment. Inline execution, preserve unrelated changes, no commit/push.

Design: Membership owns optional organizational department and authorized landing preference. Department is not an implicit authorization boundary; customer/facility/assigned-task restrictions and permission gates remain authoritative. A new Technician user may atomically create a technician profile or link a free existing profile. People detail separates account/access, skills/certifications, recurring calendar, published roster, and confirmed bookings/tasks. Multi-role people choose an authorized starting page; otherwise persona priority is Owner, Admin-only, Planner, QC, Sales, Engineer, Warehouse, Buyer, Technician, Customer, then accessible-menu fallback.
